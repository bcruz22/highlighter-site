Beta on TestFlight Highlighter

Your game, cut to the beat.

Pick the clips and photos from the match. Highlighter finds the big moments, lands every one on the beat of your song, and adds the slow-mo, punch-ins and freezes a pro editor would. Then post it to Instagram with the same song.

01 · Pick

Your clips and photos

Choose them from Photos. Highlighter finds the strike, save or tackle in each clip.

02 · Cut

On the beat, automatically

Pick a song and the part you want. The best play lands on the drop and every cut lands on a beat. Tap Auto-Edit for a fresh take.

03 · Polish

Effects that hit

Slow-mo, punch-ins, freezes, a spotlight on your player, color looks, and the real game sound.

04 · Post

With the song in Instagram

Export, share, and add the same song at the start time shown. It's copied for you.
