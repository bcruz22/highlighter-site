Privacy policy

Your reels stay yours

Highlighter turns the photos and videos you choose into highlight reels cut to the beat of a song, on your iPhone. It's made by CruzLogic. Last updated October 14, 2026.

No accountsNothing to sign up for.
No trackingNo ads or analytics.
Stays on your phoneYour reels, photos and settings.

What Highlighter uses

  • Photos and videos you pick. Highlighter reads them on your device to build your reel. Finished reels and photos are saved back to your photo library only when you export them.
  • Its own working copies, on your phone. To edit smoothly, Highlighter keeps smaller preview copies of the clips you add and a prepared copy of each photo. They stay on your phone, aren't included in your iCloud backups, and can be cleared any time in Highlighter's Settings › Storage.
  • Apple Music, if you allow it. Highlighter plays songs from your Apple Music subscription so you can hear your reel with the music. It doesn't read or change your library.
  • A short diagnostic log, on your phone. When something goes wrong, like an export that fails or a crash, Highlighter notes which step failed, the error code, the app version, the iPhone model and the free space. It never notes your photos, videos, names, reel titles or songs. The log stays on your phone. It's only sent if you tap Send a Problem Report in Highlighter's Settings and choose where to share it yourself.
  • Song search and recognition. Song titles you search for go to Apple's music catalog. Apple's 30-second song previews are matched with Apple's ShazamKit to find where they sit in the full song. Neither includes your photos, videos or personal information.

What Highlighter doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It doesn't upload your photos, videos or reels anywhere.

Your choices

You can change Highlighter's access to your photos and to Apple Music at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security. Highlighter's Settings › Storage clears its working copies and any clips you no longer use. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Your photos and videos stay in your library.

Questions

Send questions about this policy to the feedback email listed with Highlighter in TestFlight or the App Store, or through TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback.
