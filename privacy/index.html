Privacy policy

Highlighter

Highlighter turns the photos and videos you choose into highlight reels cut to the beat of a song, on your iPhone. It's made by CruzLogic. Last updated October 12, 2026.

No accountsNothing to sign up for.
No trackingNo ads or analytics.
Stays on your phoneYour reels, photos and settings.

What Highlighter uses

  • Photos and videos you pick. Highlighter reads them on your device to build your reel. Finished reels and photos are saved back to your photo library only when you export them.
  • Apple Music, if you allow it. Highlighter plays songs from your Apple Music subscription so you can hear your reel with the music. It doesn't read or change your library.
  • Song search and recognition. Song titles you search for go to Apple's music catalog. Apple's 30-second song previews are matched with Apple's ShazamKit to find where they sit in the full song. Neither includes your photos, videos or personal information.

What Highlighter doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It doesn't upload your photos, videos or reels anywhere.

Your choices

You can change Highlighter's access to your photos and to Apple Music at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Your photos and videos stay in your library.

Questions
